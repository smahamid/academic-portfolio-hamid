THESIS • 2025ESGSUSTAINABLE FINANCE

Navigating the Path to Sustainable Finance in Bangladesh: IPDC's Commitment to ESG Principles

S. M. Abdul Hamid • BBA Thesis, University of Chittagong • IPDC Finance PLC case study

This academic project examines how IPDC Finance PLC integrates Sustainable Finance and Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) principles into its business operations and financing activities. It explores the institution's contribution to green financing, corporate sustainability, responsible financial practices and inclusive economic development in Bangladesh, highlighting how financial institutions can promote long-term sustainability by aligning financial decisions with environmental responsibility, social inclusion and good corporate governance.

RESEARCH PROJECT • 5TH SEMESTERSME

Challenges Facing Entrepreneurship and Small Businesses in Bangladesh: Barriers to Business Growth and Sustainability

S. M. Abdul Hamid • Entrepreneurship & Small Business Management • Sources: Bangladesh Bank, BBS, World Bank, ADB, SME Foundation (2019 onward)

Small businesses are the backbone of Bangladesh's economy, yet many entrepreneurs struggle to grow and survive. Using a descriptive secondary-data approach across six barrier groups — financial, market, operational, human-resource, technology and regulatory — the study finds access to affordable finance and weak financial management are the most consistent constraints. It recommends collateral-light SME financing, financial literacy and digital training, simpler procedures, stronger support services and better record-keeping. Skills demonstrated: secondary research, literature review, data synthesis, academic writing, policy-oriented recommendations.

R PROJECTESG FORECAST 2023–2028

Projected ESG Score Trend (2023–2028) — Bangladeshi Financial Sector

S. M. Abdul Hamid • R Programming • ggplot2, time-series projection • 7 banks & NBFIs on a 100-point scale

An R-based statistical analysis and forecasting project evaluating ESG scores across seven major banks and NBFIs in Bangladesh, combining historical data with a 3-year predictive projection. IDLC Finance leads at ~96/100, followed by IPDC Finance at ~93.5/100; BRAC Bank holds ~72/100 and City Bank ~52/100, while LankaBangla, Premier Bank and Green Delta show substantial upside as ESG reporting matures. Forecast lines 2026–2028 indicate steady institutional positioning.

POWER BIRIDE-SHARING

Executive Overview of Bangladeshi Ride-Sharing Platforms — Uber, Pathao, inDrive & OBHAI

S. M. Abdul Hamid • Power BI, DAX & Power Query • Dynamic Platform & City slicers
